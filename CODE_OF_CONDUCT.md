# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project follows a professional, respectful collaboration standard. All participants in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project spaces are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in all communication.
- Focus on technical substance — critique ideas, not people.
- Welcome contributors from any background.
- Accept that maintainers may decline contributions that are out of scope.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruptive behavior after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this standard. Repeat or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to `conduct@fusionlayer.app`. Reports are treated confidentially.
